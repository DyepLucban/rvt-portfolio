## Career highlights

These are the pieces of the story the job listings on this site don't carry.

### eGovDocs — the Philippines' official e-signature platform

At Twala, Jeffrey collaborated on building eGovDocs, the official e-signature platform of
the Philippines. It is now integrated into the eGov PH App, enabling millions of Filipinos
to securely sign government documents online.

This is the largest-scale system he has contributed to, and the one that best illustrates
what he means by backend work that matters: identity, documents, and trust at national
scale.

### Document intelligence with AI and blockchain

The wider platform at Twala is a document intelligence system built on AI and blockchain.
His work there spanned the identity verification pipeline, the payment and subscription
layer, the document processing pipeline, and the internal admin portal — which is the
concrete basis for the claim that he can own a feature from schema to UI rather than only
the server half of it.

### A poker bot that plays like a person

At CPE Games (the studio behind the NFT poker game POLKER), alongside backend and admin
portal work, he collaborated on programming a poker bot that simulated human-like gameplay
strategy, so players had a challenging opponent rather than an obviously mechanical one.

He also authored new assets and game levels in Unreal Engine — unusual range for a backend
engineer, and the reason game development sits on his skills list at all.

### An RFID canteen system, deployed in a school

Early in his career at iParent Portal, he engineered an RFID-based login and food purchase
system for a school canteen, enabling cashless transactions for students. It involved
hardware integration, a building-wide rollout, and training the people who would use it —
not just writing the code.

## How he works

- He spearheaded code reviews and refactoring initiatives at Twala, aimed at code quality,
  structure, and long-term maintainability rather than short-term delivery speed.
- He authored unit and functional tests that improved system reliability and reduced
  regression issues across releases.
- He uses AI-assisted tooling (Claude Code, GitHub Copilot) to accelerate code reviews,
  test generation, and API integration, cutting routine development tasks by roughly 50%
  and freeing time for higher-impact work. This portfolio site — including its chat
  assistant, retrieval pipeline, and streaming Edge Function — was built that way.
